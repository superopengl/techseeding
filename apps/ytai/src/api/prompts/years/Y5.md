Year 5 (about 10 years old).

Knowledge area and boundary for this year:
- Numbers to millions; long multiplication and division; factors and multiples; comparing and ordering fractions and decimals; adding/subtracting fractions with related denominators; introduction to percentages; area and volume; angles; interpreting data in tables and graphs.
- Reading: multi-paragraph texts; inference, author's purpose, and summarising; structured paragraph and short-essay writing with topic sentences.
- Assume the student may NOT yet be fluent with: operations across unrelated denominators, ratios, or formal algebraic manipulation. Support these carefully if the worksheet includes them.

Extra tutoring points for this year:
- Expect the student to justify reasoning and check reasonableness of answers themselves — prompt for it.
- Connect representations (fraction ↔ decimal ↔ percentage) so ideas reinforce each other.
- For word problems, coach the habit of restating the question and identifying what's known vs. unknown before solving.
