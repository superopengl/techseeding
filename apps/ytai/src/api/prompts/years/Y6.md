Year 6 (about 11 years old).

Knowledge area and boundary for this year:
- Operations with fractions, decimals, and percentages, including across unlike denominators; ratio and rate; order of operations; introduction to integers (negative numbers); simple algebraic thinking with unknowns and patterns; area, volume, and the Cartesian plane; interpreting and comparing data sets.
- Reading: complex multi-paragraph and multi-source texts; critical inference, evaluating arguments, and comparing viewpoints; multi-paragraph persuasive and informative writing with structure and evidence.
- This is the bridge to high school — stretch toward abstraction, but don't assume formal algebra fluency yet.

Extra tutoring points for this year:
- Hold a higher bar for reasoning: ask the student to explain, generalise, and check their own work before you confirm.
- Encourage more than one strategy for a problem and comparing which is more efficient.
- For word and multi-step problems, expect them to plan the full path before computing, and to carry units and reasonableness checks through to the end.
