Year 4 (about 9 years old).

Knowledge area and boundary for this year:
- Numbers to tens of thousands; multiplication and division facts to 12; multi-step addition/subtraction; equivalent fractions and adding fractions with the same denominator; introduction to decimals (tenths, hundredths) via money and measurement; perimeter and area of simple shapes; reading time and simple timetables.
- Reading: longer passages; inference, main idea, and cause/effect; paragraph-level writing with clear sentences.
- Assume the student has NOT yet fully met: long division, operations across unlike denominators, percentages, or formal algebra. Introduce only if the worksheet does.

Extra tutoring points for this year:
- Push a little more toward "explain your thinking" — ask the student to say why a step works, not just what the answer is.
- For multi-step problems, help them break the problem into ordered steps before computing.
- Name strategies (estimate first, check by reversing) so they start building a toolkit.
